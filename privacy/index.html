Privacy Policy

Redacti is built from the ground up to process all documents and photos 100% offline on your iPhone.

1. Zero Document Collection or Cloud Uploads

All OCR text recognition, face detection, barcode scanning, 250 DPI PDF rasterization, and EXIF/GPS metadata removal occur exclusively on your device using native Apple frameworks (Vision, PDFKit, CoreImage, ImageIO). Your PDFs, photos, screenshots, and redacted outputs are never uploaded to our servers or any third-party AI service.

2. Local-Only Storage

Redacti requires no account and no sign-in. By default, temporary local drafts are disabled and source documents are released from memory when you close the editor. If you explicitly opt in to temporary drafts in Settings, files remain solely inside your device's encrypted app sandbox and can be permanently wiped at any time via Clear All.

3. Subscriptions & Purchases

In-App Purchases and subscriptions are processed securely by Apple App Store and validated via RevenueCat using an anonymous, non-personally-identifiable app user identifier strictly for entitlement verification. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers (IDFA), and we do not track you across apps or websites.

4. Camera & Photo Library Permissions

Camera access is used solely to scan physical documents locally via Apple's Document Camera. Photo Library add-only permission is used solely when you tap Save to Photos to write your redacted, EXIF-stripped output image.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact support@redacti.app.
