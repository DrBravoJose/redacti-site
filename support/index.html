Frequently Asked Questions

Does Redacti upload my PDFs or photos to the internet?
No. 100% of OCR, face/QR detection, PDF flattening, and EXIF stripping runs offline on your iPhone.

Can redacted text in exported PDFs be copied or revealed?
No. Redacti rasterizes every PDF page at 250 DPI with your masks permanently burned into pixels and rebuilds a flat PDF with zero underlying text stream and stripped metadata.

How do I restore my Pro purchase or cancel a subscription?
Tap Restore Purchases inside Settings or the Paywall screen. To manage or cancel an active subscription, open iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
